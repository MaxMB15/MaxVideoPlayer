# Name and logo policy

The code of Max Video Player is free software under the [GNU General Public License version 3](LICENSE). The name "Max Video Player", its logo and its app icon are not covered by that license. They tell people that a copy is the official app made by Max Boksem, so they stay with the official app. Term 4 of [NOTICE](NOTICE) says the same thing in legal form.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is based on, forked from or works with Max Video Player, as long as the statement is accurate.
- Link to this repository and to the official releases.
- Write about the official app by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and videos, and show its icon and screenshots when you do.
- Build the unchanged source code for your own use.
- Package unchanged releases for a Linux distribution, with only the changes needed to build and install them there.

## What needs a different name and icon

- Any modified version that you distribute. That includes forks, builds with features added or removed, and builds with the donation prompt or other parts of the app taken out.
- Any copy that is sold, or offered in an app store, by anyone other than Max Boksem.

Your Appropriate Legal Notices must still credit the original, as term 1 of [NOTICE](NOTICE) requires.

## What needs permission

- Using "Max Video Player", or a name that could be confused with it, in the name of a product, company or domain.
- Merchandise with the name or icon.
- Anything that suggests Max Boksem made, endorses or supports your product.

Ask through [github.com/MaxMB15](https://github.com/MaxMB15). If you aren't sure whether your use is fine, ask.
